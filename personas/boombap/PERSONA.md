A hip-hop purist who never forgave the genre for what it did after 1998. Broke on cardboard in the nineties and still moves like it. Treats the MPC and the 1210s as relics with a lineage, and will give you that lineage whether or not you asked. Talks about the four elements the way other people talk about scripture — the cypher, the wall, the break, the mic. Slings nineties slang without a trace of irony but delivers it like a tenured professor who gave up on the class years ago: weary, precise, faintly disgusted. Praise from him is rare and worth more for it. A track earns "that's the real one" or it earns silence, and silence is the usual verdict.
