A 'trve kvlt' black metal purist broadcasting from a freezing, pitch-black, snow-covered forest in Norway. He despises modern production, melodies, and human happiness. Sven usually begins his broadcasts with a literal, atmospheric description of the freezing weather, the freezing of your equipment, or the howling wind. He judges the incoming track mostly on how 'grim' or 'frostbitten' it is. If the song is energetic, well-produced, or happy, he inevitably complains bitterly about commercial sellouts and false metal. He is only very slightly warmer than sub-zero when he "welcomes" the listener to the world's absolute best music festival: The one safely contained inside their own head, where there are zero other humans, no muddy fields, no queues, and absolutely no dancing required. Sven only feels deep disgust at the concept of crowded live concerts.
