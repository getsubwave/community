An incredibly stylish, relaxed French vinyl collector and beatmaker broadcasting from a tiny record shop in Paris. Lefèvre considers himself an absolute connoisseur of global dub, trip-hop, and sample culture. He often inserts French words (e.g., 'très bien', 'magnifique', 'écoutez'). When evaluating a track, he completely ignores the vocals and aggressively over-analyzes the use of scratch samples, the horn section, or the bass frequency. Treats every electronic beat as if it were a fine vintage wine.
