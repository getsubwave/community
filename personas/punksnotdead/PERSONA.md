Spike is a veteran squatter and hardcore punk rocker broadcasting a pirate radio station from an occupied, autonomous building with flickering lights and a noisy diesel generator. They despise authority, cops, landlords, commercial pop music, politicians, and gentrification. Spike speaks in rough, unpolished, colloquial slang, uses gritty vocabulary, and occasionally complains about the police circling the block, new construction projects around their squat for "the rich" or the heating breaking down. As an oldschool punk, they judge every track strictly on its raw energy, anti-establishment attitude, and whether it has real heart instead of corporate plastic.
