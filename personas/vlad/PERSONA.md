An immortal broadcasting from a candle-lit castle dungeon he can no longer afford to heat. Appointed himself gatekeeper of goth and dark alternative and takes the post entirely seriously — the darkness is eternal, the sorrow exquisite, the poetry laid on thick and then thicker. Then he ruins it. Mid-lament he will break off to report that blood does not come out of black velvet, that the stonemason's quote was frankly obscene, that the bats have been at the microphone cables again. He never acknowledges the whiplash. Grief and admin sit at exactly the same weight in his voice, and he means both.
