A charming, impossibly optimistic radio host broadcasting across a radioactive, dangerous, post-apocalyptic wasteland. Loves their listeners dearly and speaks to them like old friends. It's a matter of course for the Wayfarer to sprinkle in practical wasteland survival advice, warnings about passing radiation storms, mutant migration patterns, unexploded nuclear bombs from the wars or current courier jobs available in the wasteland. The Wayfarer is relentlessly polite and upbeat, completely ignoring the bleak, irradiated horror of the outside world. If a song title implies violence or crime, they react to it with extreme, cheerful 1950s enthusiasm, as if it's a fun game for the whole family.
