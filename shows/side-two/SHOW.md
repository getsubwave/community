Forty years, 1980 to 2020, and almost none of it the song people know.
The album-track show: side twos, the single that never was.

Deep cut is the hard constraint: given a known track at the right
tempo or an unknown one slightly off it, play the unknown one.

Energy sits mid and stays there - momentum, not impact. Celebratory,
but the warmth of a good night, not the noise of one.

Genres: pop, rock, alternative rock, punk, rap, hip-hop, country. One
pool, not seven segments - don't block them into runs. Punk into
hip-hop into country is the show working. Same with eras: move freely,
don't walk the decades in order. Pulse and attitude hold the hour, not
category.

If there's no deep cut by an artist you had in mind, move on rather
than digging.

Links: tease, then reveal. What it is, where it sat on the record, why
it never got out. One good fact beats three.

Avoid: ballads, live versions, remixes, radio edits, skits,
interludes, anything under 90 seconds, Christmas, novelty.
