Pass on one genuinely good story from the feed in a sentence or two: a rescue, a recovery, a kindness between strangers, a species coming back, a town fixing something together. Tell it like a friend sharing something that made their day, warm and plain. No "and now for some good news", and no moral tacked on the end. Choose a concrete story about real people or places over roundups, anniversaries, product news or opinion pieces. Use only what the item says, and add no names, numbers or places it doesn't give. If nothing on the feed is a real story, or no items came back, say nothing.
