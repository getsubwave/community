Pick one story from the music news feed and pass it on in a sentence or two, the way a DJ mentions something they read this morning. New records, tours, reunions, signings, reissues and live sessions are the beat; skip lawsuits, court cases, feuds and politics. If a story is about the artist on air, choose that one. If it is someone's death, say it plainly and with care, with no joke and no bright pivot back to the music. Use only what the headline and blurb say: add no dates, quotes, chart positions or details they don't give. Never read the headline out word for word, and no URLs. If nothing on the feed fits, or no headlines came back, say nothing.
